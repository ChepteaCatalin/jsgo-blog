# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 21, 2026_

The short version: this blog does not use cookies, does not run analytics, does not show ads, and does not track you. There is no account system, no comment section, and no newsletter. This page explains the little that does happen when you visit, as required by the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Who is responsible

This blog is operated by Cătălin Cheptea (the “data controller” within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR). For any privacy-related questions or requests, you can email me at [vested.slump_6o@icloud.com](mailto:vested.slump_6o@icloud.com) or reach me via <a href="https://github.com/ChepteaCatalin" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub</a> or <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/catalin-cheptea" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LinkedIn</a>.

## Server logs

Like virtually every website, the hosting infrastructure that serves this blog may temporarily process technical data in server logs when you request a page: your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested URL, the referrer, and your browser’s user-agent string.

This processing is based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in delivering the site securely and reliably, and in detecting and preventing abuse. Log data is not used to identify you, is not combined with other data, and is not shared with anyone. It is retained only for as long as the hosting provider’s standard log rotation requires.

## Local storage (theme preference)

If you use the light/dark mode toggle, your choice is saved in your browser’s `localStorage` so the site remembers it on your next visit. This value never leaves your device, is not a cookie, is not transmitted to any server, and contains no personal data. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

## Fonts

All fonts are self-hosted and served from the same domain as this blog. No requests are made to Google Fonts or any other third-party font service when you visit.

## External links

This blog links to external sites such as GitHub and LinkedIn. Those links are plain links — nothing is loaded from those services when you view a page here. Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of the destination site applies.

## Cookies and consent

This site sets no cookies — neither its own nor third-party ones. Because nothing beyond what is strictly necessary is stored on your device, no consent banner is required or shown.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- access the personal data held about you (Art. 15),
- have inaccurate data rectified (Art. 16),
- have your data erased (Art. 17),
- restrict processing (Art. 18),
- data portability (Art. 20),
- object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21),
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example the data protection authority in your EU member state.

In practice, this blog stores no personal data about you beyond the transient server logs described above, so there is usually nothing to access, correct, or delete — but you are welcome to reach out with any request or question.

## Changes to this policy

If the way this blog works ever changes (for example, if analytics or a comment system is added), this policy will be updated before those changes go live, and the “last updated” date above will reflect it.
