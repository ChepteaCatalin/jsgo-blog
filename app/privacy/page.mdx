# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

The short version: this blog does not use cookies, does not run analytics, does not show ads, and does not track you. There is no account system, no comment section, and no newsletter. This page explains the little that does happen when you visit.

This policy is written to meet the requirements of:

- Law No. 195 of 25 July 2024 on personal data protection of the Republic of Moldova (the “Moldovan Law”), applicable since 23 August 2026, and
- the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the “GDPR”), to the extent it applies.

The Moldovan Law closely follows the structure of the GDPR, so the article numbers cited below are the same in both, unless stated otherwise.

For any privacy-related questions or requests, email me at [vested.slump_6o@icloud.com](mailto:vested.slump_6o@icloud.com). No data protection officer has been appointed, as none is required for a site of this kind.

## Server logs (hosting)

This blog is hosted by Vercel Inc. (United States). Like virtually every website, the hosting infrastructure processes some technical data when you request a page. This includes your IP address, an approximate location derived from it (city and country), the date and time of the request, the requested URL, the referrer, and your browser’s user-agent string.

- **Purpose:** delivering the pages you request, keeping the site secure and reliable, and detecting and preventing abuse (for example, denial-of-service attacks).
- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) of the Moldovan Law and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). My legitimate interest is to make the site available and keep it secure. The data involved is minimal and is never used to identify, profile, or track you.
- **Retention:** request logs available to me through Vercel are kept for at most 1 hour and are then deleted automatically. Vercel may keep its own infrastructure and security logs for longer, as described in the <a href="https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Vercel Privacy Notice</a>.
- **Is it required?** Your browser has to send your IP address to load any web page, so this data is necessary for technical reasons. You are under no legal or contractual obligation to provide it, but without it the site cannot be shown to you.

Log data is not combined with other data and is not sold or shared with anyone except as described in this policy.

## Local storage (theme preference)

If you use the light/dark mode toggle, your choice (`light`, `dark`, or `system`) is saved in your browser’s `localStorage` under the key `theme`, so the site remembers it on your next visit. Nothing is stored unless you use the toggle.

This value never leaves your device, is not a cookie, is not transmitted to any server, and contains no personal data. Storing it is strictly necessary to provide a feature you explicitly asked for, so no consent is required. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

## Contacting me

If you contact me by email, GitHub, or LinkedIn, I process the information you send me (such as your name, email address or username, and the content of your message) in order to reply.

- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in answering your message (Art. 6(1)(f)). If your message is a request to exercise your data protection rights, the legal basis is the legal obligation to handle such requests (Art. 6(1)(c)).
- **Retention:** your message is kept only as long as needed to deal with it and is then deleted. The exception is when I need to keep it to show how a data protection request was handled, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
- **Recipients:** your message is processed by the service you use to send it. For email, that is Apple (iCloud Mail). For GitHub or LinkedIn, that is the respective platform, under its own privacy policy.

## Recipients and international transfers

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone, except for:

Vercel is based in the United States and serves this site through a global network of servers, so your data may be processed outside the Republic of Moldova, the European Union, and the European Economic Area. Standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission are among the safeguards recognised by Art. 46 of the Moldovan Law. For more details, see the <a href="https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Vercel Privacy Notice</a>.

Personal data may also be disclosed to public authorities where this is required by law.

## Fonts and other resources

All fonts, images, scripts, and styles are self-hosted and served from the same domain as this blog. No requests are made to Google Fonts, content delivery networks, or any other third-party service when you visit.

## External links

This blog links to external sites such as GitHub, LinkedIn, and Wikipedia. Those links are plain links: nothing is loaded from those services when you view a page here. Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of the destination site applies.

## Cookies and consent

This site sets no cookies, neither its own nor third-party ones. It uses no analytics, advertising, social media plugins, or tracking technologies of any kind. Because nothing beyond what is strictly necessary is stored on your device, no consent banner is required or shown.

## Automated decision-making

No automated decision-making or profiling takes place (Art. 22 of the Moldovan Law and Art. 22 GDPR).

## Children

This blog is a general-audience site. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children and does not rely on consent for any processing.

## Security

The site is served exclusively over encrypted HTTPS connections. Because it is a statically generated site with no database, no user accounts, and no forms, no personal data about visitors is stored by the blog itself.

## Your rights

Under the Moldovan Law and the GDPR, you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data held about you (Art. 15),
- have inaccurate data **rectified** (Art. 16),
- have your data **erased** (Art. 17),
- **restrict** processing (Art. 18),
- **data portability**, where processing is based on consent or a contract (Art. 20). This does not apply to any processing on this site, which is based on legitimate interest or legal obligation,
- **object** at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21),
- not be subject to a decision based solely on **automated processing** (Art. 22).

To exercise any of these rights, email me at the address above. Requests are free of charge. I will respond without undue delay and in any case within one month of receiving your request. That period may be extended by up to two further months for complex requests, in which case you will be informed within the first month (Art. 12). I may ask for information to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

In practice, this blog stores no personal data about you beyond the short-lived server logs described above. There is usually nothing to access, correct, or delete, but you are welcome to reach out with any request or question.
